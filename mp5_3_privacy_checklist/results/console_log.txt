--- Prioritised remediation plan (P1 = sensitive data, third-party sharing, minors) ---
  1. [P1] Replace bundled-terms, implied and opt-out consent with a separate, un-ticked opt-in toggle per purpose (location-for-ads, ad ID, analytics, insurer programme); log timestamp, policy version and purpose; refusing must not block the core fitness features.
       fixes D1, G1, L1 for P2, P4, P6, P7 under DPDP Act, GDPR, LGPD
  2. [P1] Add an age gate. Require verifiable parental consent below the applicable age (GDPR Art. 8: 13-16 by member state; DPDP Act: under 18; CCPA/CPRA: opt-in to sale below 16), and switch off advertising, tracking and behavioural profiling for minors.
       fixes C4, D3, G5 for APP, P2, P4, P6, P7 under CCPA/CPRA, DPDP Act, GDPR
  3. [P1] Add a dedicated 'explicit consent' screen for health and biometric data that names the data, purpose and recipients, is separate from the Terms of Service and can be refused without losing core features.
       fixes G2, L2 for P4, P8 under GDPR, LGPD
  4. [P1] Show an opt-in prompt BEFORE precise location is collected and use a second, separate opt-in for advertising uses; default to coarse location for any ad purpose.
       fixes G2 for P2 under GDPR
  5. [P1] Add 'Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information' and 'Limit the Use of My Sensitive Personal Information' links (in the app settings and on the web), and honour the Global Privacy Control browser signal.
       fixes C2, C3, C6 for APP, P2, P4, P7, P8 under CCPA/CPRA
  6. [P1] Stop selling or sharing health data with the insurer. If the programme is kept, make it user-initiated (the user exports and sends their own data) with explicit consent, a financial-incentive notice and no data flowing by default.
       fixes L3 for P4 under LGPD
  7. [P2] Update the notice at collection / privacy notice so every category, purpose, recipient (including the insurer partner) and retention period is listed before collection.
       fixes C1, D1, G4, L5 for P4 under CCPA/CPRA, DPDP Act, GDPR, LGPD
  8. [P2] Define and enforce a finite retention period for every category (e.g. delete biometric templates on account deletion or when the leaderboard feature is switched off); implement automatic purge jobs and publish the periods.
       fixes C5, D4, G3, L4 for P8 under CCPA/CPRA, DPDP Act, GDPR, LGPD
  9. [P2] Sign data-processing agreements (GDPR Art. 28 / LGPD operator contracts / DPDP processor contracts) with the analytics vendor and ad network, and document the legal basis for any international transfer.
       fixes G4 for P2, P6, P7 under GDPR
  10. [P3] Make withdrawing consent as easy as giving it (one settings toggle per purpose, effective immediately) and publish a grievance channel that answers within the statutory period (DPDP Rules: no later than 90 days).
       fixes D5 for APP under DPDP Act

notes: DPDP Rules 2025 were notified in Nov 2025; the substantive duties (notice, consent, retention) apply from May 2027, so the
DPDP column is a forward-looking check. Under the DPDP Act there is no separate 'sensitive data' category - risk is handled via consent,
children's rules and Significant Data Fiduciary duties. GDPR does not list precise location as an Art. 9 category; the unit's rule (opt-in
before location collection) reflects regulators' high-risk treatment and ePrivacy rules.
